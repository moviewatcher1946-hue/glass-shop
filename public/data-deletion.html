How to delete your data

You can ask us to delete your account and the personal information we hold about you. This applies whether you signed up with a password or with Google.

Steps

  1. Send an email to ravencastillo258@gmail.com from the address linked to your account (or write to us in the shop chat while signed in).
  2. Put "Delete my data" in the subject line and include your username.
  3. We may ask you to confirm your identity before we act, to protect your account.
  4. We will delete your account and your personal information within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

Your account details, favorites, custom requests, chat messages you sent, loyalty points history, and order alerts subscriptions. Some records are kept for longer where the law requires it, such as order and tax records. Those are kept only for that purpose and then deleted. See our Privacy Policy for details.
