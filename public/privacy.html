Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

1. Who is responsible

Raven Castillo (Snackify) is the personal information controller for this site. Our data protection contact is Raven Castillo, ravencastillo258@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

WhatWhy
Account details: username, a password stored only in scrambled form, and your role (customer, seller or admin)To let you sign in and place orders
Orders: items, quantities, prices, totals, status, cash on delivery, and any delivery notes you writeTo prepare, deliver and record your order, and for our accounts
Messages to the shop team, and messages with other customers or sellersTo answer your questions and handle problems
Custom requests and your favoritesTo handle the request and show your saved items
Loyalty points: earned points and historyTo run the loyalty programme
Order alerts: the push subscription your browser or phone gives usTo send order updates, only if you turn them on
Security records: sign-in attempts, blocked requests and IP addressesTo keep accounts and the site safe
Device settings stored on your phone or computer: your sign-in session, cart, favorites and display optionsSo the site works the way you left it

3. AI assistant

When you use the Snackify AI assistant, the words you type, and the part of the screen you are looking at (for example the page name, your cart, and an open product), are sent to the AI provider that powers it, which may be Google, Groq, OpenRouter, NVIDIA or another provider we have chosen. Voice reading uses Google's text-to-speech. Do not type passwords, card numbers or other secrets into the assistant. Assistant conversations are not used to train AI models by us, but the providers have their own policies, which you can read on their websites.

4. Why we use it and the legal basis

We use your information to take and deliver orders, keep your account secure, answer your messages, run loyalty and alerts you asked for, and meet our legal duties (for example tax and consumer records). Where we ask for consent, such as for order alerts, you can withdraw it at any time.

5. Who we share it with

We share only what is needed with: delivery staff (name, items, delivery notes and payment due), our hosting and database provider Render (hosting and database), the AI and voice providers described above, and the push service run by your browser or phone maker. We do not sell your personal information.

6. How long we keep it

Account and order records: 5 years, to meet tax and record rules. Chat messages: 1 year. Security records: 90 days. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete or anonymize what we no longer need to keep. Some records may be kept longer where the law requires it.

7. Cookies and similar technology

We do not use advertising cookies. The site uses your browser's local storage to keep your sign-in session, cart, favorites and display settings on your device. You can clear them at any time from your browser settings, which will sign you out.

8. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you can ask to see your information, correct it, object to some uses, ask us to delete it or move it, withdraw consent, and complain to the National Privacy Commission. To use these rights, contact ravencastillo258@gmail.com. We may ask you to confirm who you are first. You can also turn off order alerts and clear your loyalty history from your dashboard, or ask us to do so.

9. Keeping it safe

We use encryption in transit, scrambled passwords, role-based access for staff, audit logs, and limits on repeated sign-in attempts. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your password private. If we learn of a breach that affects you, we will tell you and the National Privacy Commission as the law requires.

10. Children

The site is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. For major changes we will tell you on the site.
